# 35. Business Rules Catalog

## Core Business Domain Rules

1. **Security Guard Self-Registration Lock**: Security guards CANNOT self-register via the app. Guard accounts MUST be pre-provisioned by a Society Admin in `/app/staff`.
2. **Mandatory Residency Verification**: Self-registered mobile residents CANNOT access society features (visitor logs, complaints, amenities) until their account is approved (`status: 'active'`) by their local RWA Admin.
3. **Multi-Tenant Isolation**: A user belonging to Society A (`SOC-001`) MUST NOT be able to view, query, or modify documents belonging to Society B (`SOC-002`).
4. **Immutable Audit Trail**: Audit log entries (`societies/{id}/audit_logs`) generated by Cloud Functions or system actions CANNOT be edited or deleted by web/mobile clients.
5. **Super Admin Platform Authority**: Super Admin (`mohammedfaisalhasan@gmail.com`) holds master authority to create societies, view global leads, and manage SaaS campaigns.
