🏢 GateLink — The Modern Operating System for Gated Communities & Smart Apartment Living

GateLink is India’s premier smart society management platform, built to simplify, secure, and modernize apartment life. From real-time gate security and instant visitor approvals to zero-hassle digital maintenance payments and amenity bookings, GateLink brings your entire residential community onto a single, intuitive mobile dashboard.

Whether you are a flat owner, resident, tenant, or managing committee member, GateLink empowers you with effortless control over your home safety, society dues, and community interactions.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🌟 KEY FEATURES & HIGHLIGHTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🚪 1. ADVANCED VISITOR & GATE SECURITY
• Instant Entry Alerts: Receive real-time push notifications the moment a guest, delivery partner (Zomato, Swiggy, Amazon, Blinkit), or cab (Uber, Ola) reaches the main gate.
• 1-Tap Approvals: Allow or deny visitor entry directly from your smartphone screen from anywhere in the world.
• Pre-Approved Gate Passes: Generate QR-code digital invites and share via WhatsApp with friends and family for seamless, queue-free entry.
• Delivery Management: Instruct security guards to leave parcels at the gate safely when you're away from home.

💳 2. DIGITAL SOCIETY MAINTENANCE & BILL PAYMENTS
• 1-Click UPI & Card Payments: Pay monthly maintenance fees, electricity surcharge, water bills, and sinking fund dues securely.
• Zero Convenience Fee Options: Instant payments via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, and Net Banking powered by RBI-authorized payment gateways.
• Automated Receipts & Invoices: Download GST-compliant digital payment receipts instantly.
• Dues Tracking: View historical billing statements, breakdown of maintenance charges, and upcoming payment reminders.

🛠️ 3. RESOLUTION HELPDESK & MAINTENANCE TICKETS
• Photo-Backed Complaints: Snap a picture of water leaks, electrical faults, or lift issues and raise tickets in seconds.
• Real-Time Progress Tracker: Track status from "Assigned" to "In Progress" and "Resolved" with technician contact details.
• Feedback & Ratings: Rate the quality of maintenance work to ensure high facility standards.

🏊 4. CLUBHOUSE & AMENITY RESERVATIONS
• Digital Slot Booking: Reserve the Clubhouse, Banquet Hall, Swimming Pool, Tennis Court, or Badminton Hall with live calendar availability.
• Fair Usage Rules: Automatic enforcement of society guidelines and slot timing restrictions.
• Instant Confirmations: Receive booking passes and automated slot reminder alerts.

📢 5. DIGITAL NOTICE BOARD & COMMUNITY HUB
• Instant Circulars: Read official circulars, AGM updates, and urgent notices issued by the RWA / Management Committee.
• Verified Resident Directory: Connect with neighbors without sharing private phone numbers.
• Emergency Contacts: Quick 1-tap call to Security Guard Cabin, Facility Manager, Society President, and local Emergency Services (Police, Ambulance, Fire).

👩‍🍳 6. DAILY HELP & DOMESTIC STAFF MANAGEMENT
• Live Check-In / Check-Out: Know the exact moment your maid, cook, driver, or tutor enters and exits the society gate.
• Attendance Records: View monthly attendance logs for your domestic helpers.
• Helper Ratings: Check ratings and reviews from fellow society residents before hiring home staff.

🅿️ 7. SMART VEHICLE & PARKING MANAGEMENT
• Authorized Parking: Manage your two-wheeler and four-wheeler vehicle records.
• Parking Violation Alerts: Quickly identify incorrectly parked vehicles in your designated slot and notify the owner through the app.

🔒 ENTERPRISE-GRADE PRIVACY & DATA SECURITY
