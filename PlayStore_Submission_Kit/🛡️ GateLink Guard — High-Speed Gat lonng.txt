🛡️ GateLink Guard — High-Speed Gate Security & Visitor Management Terminal

GateLink Guard is the official security terminal app designed for gatekeepers, security personnel, and facility managers in gated communities, residential societies, and commercial complexes.

Built for ultra-fast, one-tap operations, GateLink Guard replaces traditional paper registers with a digital logbook that accelerates gate check-ins, eliminates visitor bottlenecks, and safeguards residential premises.

⚡ KEY FEATURES FOR SECURITY GUARDS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🚪 1. INSTANT VISITOR CHECK-IN & APPROVALS
• One-Tap Entry: Quickly record visitor name, mobile number, visiting flat, and purpose of visit with live camera photo capture.
• Instant Resident Notification: Automatically alerts the flat resident via mobile notification to get instant Allow / Deny approval.
• Pre-Approved Gate Passes: Scan digital QR code invites generated by residents for instant entry in under 3 seconds.

📦 2. DELIVERY & CAB QUICK ENTRY
• Fast-Track Delivery Check-In: Rapid check-in presets for delivery partners (Zomato, Swiggy, Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto).
• "Leave at Gate" Tagging: Safely log parcels left at security cabin with photo proof sent directly to the resident.
• Cab Verification: Log incoming cabs (Uber, Ola) with vehicle number and destination flat tracking.

👩‍🍳 3. DAILY HELP & SERVICE STAFF TRACKING
• Digital Staff Attendance: Fast check-in/check-out for domestic helpers, maids, cooks, drivers, cleaners, and maintenance technicians.
• Passcode & ID Verification: Instant verification of authorized staff to prevent unauthorized entry of outside workers.

🚗 4. VEHICLE & PARKING LOGBOOK
• Number Plate Logging: Record incoming and outgoing visitor vehicles to prevent parking violations.
• Unauthorized Parking Lookup: Search any vehicle number to instantly find the associated flat owner or visitor details.

🚨 5. EMERGENCY SOS & PANIC ALERTS
• Instant Resident SOS Alerts: Receive high-priority audio-visual alerts when a resident triggers an in-app emergency alarm.
• Direct Intercom / Call: 1-tap direct call to Society Admin, Facility Manager, and Resident intercoms.
• Security Incident Logging: Report unusual gate incidents directly to the Society Management Committee.
