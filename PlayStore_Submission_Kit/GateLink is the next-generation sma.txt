GateLink is the next-generation smart society management and gated community security app designed to make residential living safe, seamless, and convenient.

Whether you are a resident, flat owner, or tenant, GateLink connects you directly with your society gate, committee, and neighbors in real time.

🚪 Smart Visitor Management:
• Instant push notifications when guests, delivery executives, or cabs arrive at the gate.
• Pre-approve expected guests with digital Gate Passes and QR codes.
• One-tap Allow / Deny entry for ultimate home security.

• View and pay monthly society maintenance dues instantly via UPI, Cards & Netbanking.

• Raise maintenance tickets (plumbing, electrical, common areas) with photos in seconds.
